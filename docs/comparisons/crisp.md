# Deskcord vs Crisp — Discord-Based Alternative

A **Crisp alternative** with native Discord integration. Deskcord offers customer support through Discord at $4.99/month, compared to Crisp's per-workspace pricing that grows as you add more sites or teams.

## Pricing reality check

Crisp's Pro plan costs **$25/workspace/month**. Most growing teams need multiple workspaces, pushing real costs to **$50+/month**. Deskcord covers the same ground for **$4.99–$19.99/month**, with no per-workspace surcharge.

## Why teams switch from Crisp to Deskcord

- **No per-workspace pricing** — add as many domains as you want under one subscription.
- **Discord-native workflow** — reply from an app your team is already using, instead of a separate multi-channel inbox.
- **Faster setup** — a single script tag, generated with one Discord command, live in about 5 minutes.
- **Your data stays yours** — conversations live in your own Discord server, not inside Crisp's platform.

Crisp is a strong choice if you need deep multi-channel support (email, SMS, etc.) in one inbox. If your team just needs a **live chat widget** tied to a support desk you already use, Deskcord is the simpler, cheaper path.
