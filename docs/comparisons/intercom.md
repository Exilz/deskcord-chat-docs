# Deskcord vs Intercom — Affordable Alternative for Startups

Looking for an **Intercom alternative**? Deskcord offers straightforward customer support through Discord at $4.99/month vs. Intercom's $78+/month — just the essentials, without the bloat, for roughly 90% less. It's built as an **Intercom replacement for startups** who want live chat without enterprise seat pricing.

## Pricing reality check

Intercom's cheapest plan starts at **$39/seat** with a 2-seat minimum, putting the real floor at **$78/month** — and most teams end up paying **$500+/month** once they add the features they actually need. Deskcord's Team plan, with unlimited seats, starts at **$19.99/month** (billed annually).

## Why teams switch from Intercom to Deskcord

- **No per-seat pricing** — Intercom's cost climbs with every teammate you add; Deskcord's Team plan is flat regardless of headcount.
- **No new dashboard to learn** — support happens in Discord, a tool most teams already use daily.
- **Unlimited domains** — run several products or sites from one subscription; Intercom typically charges extra per workspace.
- **Your data isn't locked in** — conversations live in your own Discord server, so cancelling doesn't mean losing your history.
- **Setup in minutes, not days** — no onboarding calls or complex configuration required.
