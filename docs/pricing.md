# Deskcord Pricing

Deskcord is flat-priced **help desk software** with no per-seat fees — a deliberate contrast to most **live chat software** and **customer service chat** tools, which charge per agent and scale cost with headcount.

## Plans

All plans include a 7-day free trial, unlimited domains, and unlimited conversations. Cancel anytime.

### Solo — $4.99/month ($4/month billed annually, $49/year)

Perfect for solo founders and indie hackers running multiple products.

- 1 support agent (you)
- Unlimited domains — use it on every site you run
- Unlimited conversations
- Full widget customization
- Mobile notifications
- 7-day free trial

### Team — $19.99/month ($16/month billed annually, $190/year)

Perfect for small teams. Most popular plan.

- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited domains — one subscription for all your sites
- Unlimited conversations
- Full widget customization
- Mobile notifications
- 7-day free trial

## How much are you saving?

Deskcord is built to be a **budget-friendly Zendesk competitor** and an **affordable alternative to Intercom** — here's the actual math against the major **help desk software** and **live chat widget** vendors:

| Tool | Typical cost | Deskcord Team (annual) |
|---|---|---|
| **Intercom** | $39/seat, 2-seat minimum → $78+/month, often $500+/month at scale | $19.99/month |
| **Zendesk** | $55/agent/month → $165/month for a 3-agent team | $19.99/month |
| **Crisp** | $25/workspace/month, $50+/month across multiple workspaces | $19.99/month |
| **Help Scout / HelpCrunch** | $15/user/month → $45/month for a 3-person team | $19.99/month |

Deskcord's cost stays flat regardless of team size, product count, or domain count — a **cheap support tool** for teams who don't want per-seat pricing to explode as they grow.

## Managing your subscription

Use `/manage-subscription` in Discord to open the Stripe customer portal, where you can upgrade, downgrade, update your payment method, view invoices, or cancel. You can also manage billing directly through the Stripe customer portal by entering the email address you used to subscribe.

## What happens if you cancel

Your Discord server and every conversation thread stay with you forever — cancelling only removes the widget from your website. Unlike most **customer support widget** vendors, your data is never locked into a proprietary platform.
