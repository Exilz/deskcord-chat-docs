# Why Discord for Customer Support?

"Isn't Discord for gamers?" It used to be — but Discord has evolved into one of the best free team communication tools available, which is exactly why it works as **customer service chat** infrastructure for Deskcord's **Discord-based customer support**.

## The case for Discord as a help desk

- **You're already using it** — personally, or with your team. There's no new tool to onboard.
- **The mobile app is excellent** — instant push notifications for new customer messages, with zero extra setup.
- **Threads keep conversations organized** — the same clean, familiar threading model as Slack.
- **It's free forever** — unlike the paid tiers of Slack or Microsoft Teams.
- **Your data stays with you** — conversations live in your own Discord server, not locked inside expensive, proprietary SaaS.

Thousands of startups already run their entire internal operations on Discord. Deskcord extends that same server to **customer support**, instead of asking you to pay for and learn a second platform.

## Discord vs Slack vs Teams for support

| Feature | Discord | Slack | Teams |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free forever | $8/user/month | $4/user/month |
| Message history | Unlimited | 90 days (free tier) | Limited |
| Threads | Built-in, clean | Available | Available |
| Mobile experience | Excellent | Good | Okay |
| Voice/Video | Included | Paid tier | Included |
| Already installed? | Likely (personal use) | Needs setup | Needs setup |

## Privacy by design

Deskcord stores zero message content on its own servers. Every customer conversation is a real Discord thread inside your own server, so Discord — not Deskcord — handles infrastructure, encryption, and compliance. If you ever cancel Deskcord, every conversation you've ever had stays in your Discord server, permanently.
